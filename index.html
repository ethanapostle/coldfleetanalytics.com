The problem

Fleet maintenance costs are tracked in spreadsheets, hunches, or not at all.

We've worked with fleets of every size and found the same situation everywhere. Maintenance data exists — it's just scattered across work orders, invoices, vendor portals, and someone's memory.

The result? Decisions made on gut feel, budgets built on guesswork, and no clear answer to the question that actually matters: why is it costing this much?

📋

No asset-level cost visibility

You know total spend, but not which assets are eating the budget or why.

🔧

Reactive maintenance cycles

Failures are discovered on the road, not in the shop. Downtime is unplanned and expensive.

📊

No benchmark to compare against

Is your cost-per-asset reasonable? There's been no honest way to know — until now.

How it works

Clear answers in three steps.

No lengthy implementations. No new software your team won't use. We work with the data you already have.

01

We connect to your data

Work orders, invoices, telematics exports — whatever you have, we ingest and normalize it. Most fleets are fully onboarded within a week.

02

We build your cost model

Every maintenance event is attributed to an asset, a component, and a cause. Failure prediction models are trained on your specific fleet history.

03

You get honest reporting

Monthly reports, live dashboards, and quarterly reviews that tell you what's driving costs — and what to do about it. In plain language.

About us

Built on the belief that fleet data should be honest.

Cold Fleet Analytics was founded by fleet operators and data engineers who were tired of seeing the same problem: expensive maintenance programs producing reports nobody trusted or acted on.

We're based in Winnipeg and work primarily with Canadian commercial fleets — refrigerated transport, cold logistics, and mixed-use commercial operators across the Prairies and beyond.

We're not a software platform. We're an analytics partner. That means a real person learns your fleet, builds your model, and reviews the numbers with you every quarter.

01

Honest numbers, always

We don't optimize reports to look good. We show you what the data says, even when it's uncomfortable — because that's the only way to improve.

02

Fleet-first thinking

Every model we build is trained on your data, not industry averages. Your fleet is unique. Your analytics should be too.

03

Decisions, not dashboards

We measure success by what changes — not by how many charts we deliver. If the data doesn't lead to action, we've failed.

04

Long relationships

The best insights emerge over time. We build multi-year partnerships because that's when the data really starts to pay off.
